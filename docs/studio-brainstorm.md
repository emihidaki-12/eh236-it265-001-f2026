## Vision Statement
*What is the mission of your solo game studio? How does it align with creating and analyzing innovative game architectures?*

> My vision for Konbini Studio is to create casual and entertaining games that anyone can easily pick up and enjoy. Like a konbini, I want the studio to offer a little bit of everything and experiment with different game ideas and mechanics but keeping it cozy, casual, relaxing and simple.

---

## Core Values
*What principles guide your studio's approach to game design, architecture, and development?*

- Accessibility: Games should be easy for new players to understand and start playing very intuitive.
- Fun: The main goal is to create games that are entertaining and making people want to come back.
- Simplicity: Keep the controls, rule and game systems simple without making the game boring.
- Experimentation: Try different mechanics, themes and ideas instead of just limiting to one type of game.

---

- **Demographic:** Casual players, students, indie game fans who just want to get distracted.

## Unique Selling Point (USP)
*What will make your games stand out from others, particularly in the indie/board game space?*

> What makes konbini studio different is the idea of having a little bit of everything. Instead of focusing on only one genre, I want to experiment with different types of games while keeping them casual and easy to play. The goal is to create games that don't require a huge time commitment or a lot of experience to understand. Someone should be able to pick up one of my games, learn the basics quickly, and have fun with it.
