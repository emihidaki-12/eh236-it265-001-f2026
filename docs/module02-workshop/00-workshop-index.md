## Selected Concept

My selected concept is **Yakin**, a multiplayer social deduction game set during the night shift of a Japanese konbini. One player is the employee, while the other players are customers inside the store. One of the customers is secretly a yokai.

The customers must work together to identify the yokai and warn the employee before it eliminates them. The employee has the ability to defeat the yokai, while the yokai tries to stay hidden, eliminate the customers, and eventually reach the employee.
