# IT265 Module 2: Peer Feedback

**Concept:** Yakin

**Reviewer initials:** JL

**In my words, the player repeatedly:**

> The players are inside the konbini with one employee and several customers. One of the customers is secretly a yokai. The customers have to observe each other and figure out who the yokai is while the yokai tries to hide its identity and eliminate the other players. The employee has the ability to defeat the yokai, but the customers have to identify the correct player and warn the employee before it is too late.

**One clarifying question:**

> How will the yokai eliminate other customers without immediately revealing its identity to everyone?

**Strongest hook or source of appeal:**

> The strongest part of the idea is having everyone trapped inside the konbini with one hidden yokai among them. It creates more tension because the players know that someone in the group is the enemy, but they do not know who it is. The yokai also has a clear final objective: eliminate the customers and eventually reach the employee.

**What one student could prototype first:**

> The first prototype could use simple role cards for one employee, several customers, and one hidden yokai. The players could test the basic deduction system and see if the customers can collect enough information to identify the yokai and warn the employee.

**Biggest uncertainty or risk to test:**

> The biggest uncertainty is how the yokai can secretly eliminate customers while still keeping its identity hidden. The game also needs enough clues for the customers to make an informed decision instead of randomly accusing each other.

**One actionable suggestion:**

> Give the employee a special ability that can defeat the yokai, but require the other customers to correctly identify the yokai and communicate that information to the employee. The yokai wins if it eliminates the other customers and reaches the employee before being identified.
