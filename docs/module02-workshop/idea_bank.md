# IT265 Module 2: Idea Bank

Use this sheet during the concept workshop. Link the completed document from your workshop index; it supports the journal and treatment rather than adding another graded submission.

List possible concepts before choosing one. Mark each as **ready to pitch**, **unclear loop**, **scope risk**, or **save for later**. Shortlist two or three ideas for the pitch round.

| Working title | Repeated player decision or action | Category | Reason or open question |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Yakin** | Players observe each other, communicate, and try to identify who is secretly a yokai. The yokai tries to blend in and avoid being discovered. | **Ready to pitch** | The social deduction idea has a clear goal and interesting player interaction. The main question is how the yokai can secretly eliminate players without immediately revealing their identity. |
| **Onigiri Dash** | The player avoids obstacles while collecting different onigiri fillings for points, bonuses, or abilities. | **Ready to pitch** | The basic gameplay is easy to understand and fits the casual style of Konbini Studio. The main question is whether collecting different fillings creates enough strategy to make it different from other endless-runner games. |
| **Konbini Cats** | Players collect food from a konbini while deciding whether to take another item or escape before getting caught. | **Save for later** | The push-your-luck mechanic could work well as a simple card game, but I want to focus on developing Yakin and Onigiri Dash first. |

**Two or three concepts to pitch:**

1. **Yakin** – A social deduction game set during the night shift of a Japanese konbini where one customer is secretly a yokai.
2. **Onigiri Dash** – A casual endless-runner game where an onigiri avoids obstacles and collects different fillings.
