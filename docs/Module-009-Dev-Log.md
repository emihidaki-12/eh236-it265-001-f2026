#### Learnings

I think this could be useful because it would allow me to organize different characters and their abilities without having to create completely separate logic for every character.
>
> I also learned more about the importance of spatial rules and how the layout of a level can influence player decisions. In YAKIN, the different areas of the convenience store are not just decorative since player positions determine who they can interact with and who the yokai can attack.
>
> Another important concept is that the user interface should help players understand the game without becoming distracting or overwhelming.
